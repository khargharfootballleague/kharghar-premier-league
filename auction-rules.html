Bidding Pipeline & Increments

Standard Bid Scale

Bidding for every targeted player will officially commence from a baseline Base Price of 2 KPL Points. Price growth follows a strict mathematical stair-step system:

From 2 to 10 Points Increases by +1 Point
From 10 to 40 Points Increases by +2 Points
Above 40 Points Increases by +3 Points

Once a player is successfully knocked down, the final bid amount is instantly deducted from the buying manager's live purse.

Round 2 Recall Rule

No talent is completely out of the tournament after a single pass. Any player who remains unselected during the initial draft rotations will automatically bypass permanent exclusion.

All unsold players will be recalled for bidding in the second round.

Specialty Card Mechanics

The Official KPL Power Card

Each team is armed with exactly 1 Power Card for the entire night. This strategic tool can override standard draft restrictions under strict constraints:

  • Activation Threshold: Can only be activated once bidding for a player successfully touches or passes 70 KPL Points.
  • Single Use Limit: A franchise can buy a player using this specific mechanic only once across the entire night.
  • Financial Step Rate: Once activated, Power Card bidding shifts entirely into cash increments of +500 ₹.

Goalkeeper Allocation Protocol

To preserve balanced roster health and ensure fair goalkeeper distributions, netminders fall under specific structural draft protocols:

Rule 1: Round 1 Limit

Each team is permitted to purchase exactly 1 Goalkeeper during the first round of the auction.

Rule 2: Emergency Option

If 5 GK are sold and only 1 GK remains, any team that has not bought a GK is given an options-clause to acquire that final player automatically for 10 KPL Points.

Rule 3: Unsold Eligibility

If any Goalkeeper passes through Round 1 completely unsold, his structural locked-status drops, making him entirely eligible for open bidding by all teams.

Rule 4: GK Playing Rule

Teams can play any player as Goalkeeper during matches.

KPL Supreme Governance Clause

The final verdict, interpretation, and structural adjustments issued by the official KPL Committee are absolute and binding. All participating managers, scouts, and stakeholders must completely obey all committee declarations.

2. Salary Cap & Auction Framework

Driven by a strict ₹330 KPL Points Salary Cap model per club, with unspent points rolling forward to future season pools.

Season 3 Retention Model

  • Max Retentions 3 Players + Owner
  • 1st Player Cost 30 KPL Pts
  • 2nd Player Cost 30 KPL Pts
  • 3rd Player Cost 70 KPL Pts

Season 4 Roster Expansion

  • Max Retentions 4 Players + Owner
  • 1st & 2nd Cost 30 Pts each
  • 3rd Player Cost 50 KPL Pts
  • 4th Player Cost 70 KPL Pts

Power Card (PC) Rule

Activates at 70 KPL points. Shifts bidding into real currency increments of ₹500.

S4 Power Card Cap

Max 2 PC players retained. If both are kept, team cannot execute a Power Card at the active auction.

***For Season 5, PC base price will start from ₹5000.

Goalkeeper Base Protection

If dedicated GK rules are activated during the draft night, the minimum base point for a GK is locked at 10 KPL points. Teams can play any player as GK during the tournament

Players maintain absolute sovereignty to refuse a club retention request, as clubs do not pay out structural player salaries for retentions.

3. Match Day & Playing Conditions

1

Duration: Two halves of 25 minutes each (50-minute games) with a flat 5-minute halftime interval.

2

Substitutions: Dynamic "Rolling Substitutions" are permitted at any time to preserve stamina.

3

Sin Bin Penalty: A Yellow Card triggers a compulsory 3-minute temporary cooling-off suspension in the Sin Bin. Red Cards command immediate and permanent expulsion.

Strategic Video Review Challenge** [TBD}

Each franchise gets exactly 1 challenge per match, strictly limited to three distinct scenarios: Goals, Straight Red Cards, or Penalty appeals.

Note: If the camera network has no recorded angle of the incident, the referee's on-field ruling stands, and the club preserves their review. Unsuccessful challenges lose the review.

Playoff Clause

A centralized master league points table is maintained. The top 4 teams step into the Knockouts (1st vs 4th & 2nd vs 3rd).

Force Majeure: If weather or unforeseen logistics disrupt Semifinals or Finals, the club holding the higher ranking on the master standings table is declared the winner.

To maintain high fan engagement and local look, at least 50% of a team's total registered roster must be physically present on the field at the opening whistle of the first match of any given match day.

6. Franchise Ownership & Lock-In Framework*** [TBD]

Tier A: Playing Franchise Owners

  • Lock-in Window: Playing owners face a compulsory lock-in period of 5 full seasons.
  • Exit Execution: Teams can be sold via league-managed auction or a flat direct sale channel.
  • Auction Floor: Bidding begins from the valuation at which the last franchise was sold by the committee.
  • Fee Deductions: A 10% fee covers administrative league share and upfront entrance costs, with the balance distributed to the outgoing owner.
  • New Owner Retentions: The incoming group retains 1 squad player for 30 KPL points, or a 2nd player for an advanced cost of 70 KPL points.
  • Direct Sale Price: Set at the price of the last team sold (e.g., ₹21k) plus active tournament costs (e.g., ₹15k), distributed 50/50 between league and old owner.

Tier B: Corporate Investors / Non-Playing Owners

Investors must nominate an active player as the official Owner Representative, triggering a mandatory 3-season locker lock-in.

Early Termination Clause: If altered before the 3-season block concludes, the team is completely dissolved. Investors can rebuild by keeping the new rep plus 1 player from the old squad for 30 points (entering the draft with a reduced pool of 270 points). If keeping just the representative, they enter the room with a standard 300 points budget.

Tier C & D: Co-Ownership Rules & Transfers

Co-Ownership Limits: Max 3 co-owners per club with a matching 5-season lock-in. Retaining two co-owners commands a baseline fee of 60 KPL points from the pool. Clubs retain an optional regular draft save at 30 points and a secondary at 70 points.

League Transfer Embargo: If an owner or co-owner leaves a squad to enter the upcoming general draft pool as a free player, their previous club is hit with an absolute bidding embargo and cannot submit a bid for them.

Official Individual Tie-Breaker System

Top Goal Scorer If tied on total conversions, the prize falls to the player holding more cumulative MVP tournament votes. If still level, the award is settled by total assists recorded.
Best Midfielder / Playmaker Evaluated by total assists. Tied scores shift to total tournament votes received, and then resolve to total individual goals scored.
Best Defender & Goalkeeper Defenders need a minimum of 6 apps, evaluated by total FPL points, then votes, then clean sheets. Keepers (Golden Gloves) are sorted by least goals conceded across at least 6 starts, then percentage per game, and finally overall votes.

The Ultimate Governance Clause: Player of the Match honors are decided entirely by match referees. Player of the Tournament is decided by a league-wide system combining total FPL metrics and non-team member peer voting. The absolute final judgment on all rules, prizes, and on-field metrics rests exclusively with the official KPL Committee.
